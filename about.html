About AZ-salestracker

AZ Sales Tracker is the smarter, faster, and cleaner way for small businesses to stay in control of their numbers.

Designed with simplicity at its core, the app helps shop owners, cafés, and independent sellers track their daily performance effortlessly , no complicated tools, no cluttered dashboards. With just a few taps, you can record sales, monitor revenue in real time, and instantly see which items are performing best. Weekly insights give you a clear picture of your business trends, helping you make smarter decisions and grow with confidence.

AZ Sales Tracker delivers a smooth, secure, and reliable experience. Every user gets their own protected space, ensuring privacy and safety from the moment they sign in. Whether you’re managing a small store or running a home business, AZ Sales Tracker gives you the clarity you need , anytime, anywhere. Simple. Fast. Intuitive. Everything your sales tracking should be.
