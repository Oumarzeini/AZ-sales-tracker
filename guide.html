How it works

AZ Sales Tracker is designed to simplify your business workflow. The app helps you move from setting up your account to tracking everyday sales, understanding revenue, and recognizing patterns in your performance.

01

Create Your Account

Start by creating an account on the authentication page. Once you sign in, your dashboard becomes your control center for everything related to your sales and business activity.

02

Add Your Products

Before you can record sales, set up the products you sell. Add each item with the right details so every transaction is organized and easy to track later.

03

Record a New Sale

When a customer makes a purchase, log the sale using the sales form. Select the product, add the quantity, and save it. The system stores the transaction so your records stay accurate.

04

Monitor Daily Revenue

Visit the daily revenue section to see how much your store is earning over time. This gives you a clear view of your business progress and helps you spot trends quickly.

05

Review the Summary Page

The summary page brings together your core business numbers in one place. You can review overall totals, recent sales, and recent performance to understand what is happening across your store.

06

Follow Weekly Insights

Use the weekly insights page to understand how your sales evolve across the week. This helps you recognize best-selling products, stronger sales days, and opportunities to improve your strategy.

The full flow

In short, the process is simple: create an account, add your products, record each sale, monitor your daily revenue, review the summary, and use weekly insights to improve your business decisions.

This gives you a complete overview of your store performance without creating chaos or confusion. Everything stays connected, and your sales data becomes easier to understand and act on.
