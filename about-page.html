About us

AZ Sales Tracker helps you stay in control of every sale.

AZ Sales Tracker was created to make sales management simpler, clearer, and more efficient for small businesses. Instead of juggling scattered records, notes, and spreadsheets, users can keep everything in one organized place.

The app is built for businesses that need a practical and reliable way to track sales, monitor revenue, and understand how their store is performing over time. It turns daily activity into useful information that helps owners make better decisions.

Simple Tracking

Record each sale quickly and access your most important numbers without extra clutter or confusion.

Clear Insights

Follow daily revenue, review summaries, and use weekly insights to spot patterns that matter for your business.

Secure & Personal

Every user has their own protected space so business data stays private, organized, and easy to manage.

Our mission

To help businesses stay organized, informed, and ready to grow.

We believe sales tracking should feel practical, not complicated. AZ Sales Tracker gives business owners a simple tool to understand what is happening in their store and make confident decisions based on real numbers.
